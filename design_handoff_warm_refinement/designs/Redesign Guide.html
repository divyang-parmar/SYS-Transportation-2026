1 What we're working with

An operations tool for getting devotees to and from the airport

During a shibir, hundreds of Bhaktos fly in and out. The app coordinates the volunteers (Sarthis) and vehicles that pick them up — grouping passengers by flight, assigning a Sarthi to each, and notifying everyone over SMS and email. Three people use it, each with very different needs.

Super Admin

Sets up the world

Manages users, vehicles, and the SMS / email notification templates. Heavy forms, infrequent use.

Desktop · dense

Transport Admin

Runs the operation

Sees every arrival & departure flight group, assigns a Sarthi to each booking, watches what's still unassigned.

Desktop · the command center

Sarthi (Driver)

Does the pickups

On a phone, in a busy terminal. Needs their pickup list, passenger contacts, and a big "done" button. Nothing else.

Mobile · glanceable

3 My honest assessment

Solid bones, generic skin

It's a genuinely capable, well-structured tool. The redesign isn't about fixing what's broken — it's about giving it an identity and tightening it for the people actually using it day-of.

✓ Keep

  • ✓Clear role-based screens — each user lands exactly where they should
  • ✓The flight-group → booking → Sarthi mental model is sound and scannable
  • ✓The login screen's warmth (cream + terracotta) is the one place the brand sings
  • ✓Sensible summary cards and progress indicators already exist

↑ Elevate

  • ↑Commit to the warmth. Carry the login's cream/terracotta through the whole app instead of defaulting to blue
  • ↑Scale up for the Sarthi. 0.72rem labels and small targets are rough on a phone in a terminal — bigger type, 48px+ hits
  • ↑Systematize status. Many ad-hoc colored pills (amber, teal, red, green) → one consistent status language
  • ↑Kill the inline hex. Hardcoded colors don't respond to dark mode — move to tokens

4 Three directions

Pick a lane — or mix and match

These aren't mutually exclusive. A is a finish we'd want regardless; B and C are bigger swings on specific screens. Tell me which screen matters most and which direction appeals, and I'll build it out fully and clickable.

● Low risk · refresh

A · Warm Refinement

Same structure, true to the brand

Keep every screen where it is, but finally deliver the design system as written: temple-warm surfaces, a single status language, a larger type scale, and proper tokens.

  • Cream + terracotta surfaces app-wide
  • Unified status chips & badges
  • Bigger, more legible type scale
  • Polished dark mode
● Medium · rethink

B · Sarthi-First

The day-of mobile pickup tool

Rebuild the driver experience as a focused, big-touch field tool: a time-ordered run of pickups, one-tap call & navigate, an oversized complete action, and an at-a-glance "where am I in my day" header.

  • Timeline of today's pickups
  • One-tap call / WhatsApp / maps
  • Oversized complete + swipe
  • Works one-handed, offline-friendly
● Higher · reimagine

C · Ops Command Board

A live timeline for the admin

Reimagine the Transport Admin as a real-time board: flights laid out on a time axis, live status (delayed / landed), unassigned bookings surfaced loudly, and drag-to-assign Sarthis with capacity awareness.

  • Flights on a time axis
  • Live status & delay surfacing
  • Drag-to-assign Sarthis
  • "What still needs a driver" front & center
